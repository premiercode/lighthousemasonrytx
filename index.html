Bring tired brick back

This illustration shows the same wall twice. On the left: efflorescence, grime and a stair-step crack. On the right: the same wall after we clean it, repoint it and seal it. Drag the slider or tap anywhere on the illustration, or tab to it and use the arrow keys.

    Low-pressure, masonry-safe wash lifts salts, rust and grime without etching the brick face.

    Cracked joints are ground out and repointed; spalled brick is replaced with a matched unit.
